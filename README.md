# T9 Numpad IME for Android

A one-handed 12-key Android keyboard combining **Gboard's 4-column Numpad ergonomics** with **OG T9 predictive text** and **ABC Multi-Tap** fallback.

## What the Keyboard Does
- **Fast One-Handed T9 Typing**: Type one tap per letter on digits `2`–`9`, cycle candidates with `#`, insert space with `0`, cycle punctuation with `1`, and hold any number key to insert a literal digit.
- **Persistent Modes & Dynamic Case Labels**: Toggle **T9** on/off next to the Settings gear, switch strictly between **123** and **ABC/T9** with the top-right key, and watch on-key labels (`abc`, `Abc`, `ABC`) update live with your active shift state.
- **Frequent Word Learning & Spell-to-Add**: Learns from the words you type frequently so preferred words rank first, plus lets you spell new words in ABC and save them straight to your T9 dictionary.
- **Back-to-Back Clipboard History**: Captures copied words and snippets back-to-back so you can tap the clipboard icon in the keyboard strip to paste any previous clip at any time.
- **Synthesized Audio & Haptics**: Real-time synthesized **Clicks**, **Beeps**, or **Piano** soundsets, adjustable keypress volume and vibration strength, opposite-theme bold key popups, 3-page Symbols, and continuous character-by-character hold-to-delete.
- **Rounded Top Profile & Hidden Launcher Icon**: Clean rounded top corners on the keyboard tray and a clean app drawer (the home launcher icon is hidden by default and can be turned on from Settings).

## How to Enable & Configure It
1. After installing the APK, open your phone's **Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard → Manage on-screen keyboards** (location varies slightly by Android device) and toggle on **T9 Numpad (One-Handed)**.
2. Tap any text field, tap the Android keyboard switcher icon in the bottom navigation bar, and choose **T9 Numpad (One-Handed)**.
3. To open **T9 Numpad Settings** at any time, tap the **gear icon** on the top-right of the keyboard strip (or open Settings from Android's On-screen keyboard menu).
4. Inside Settings, expand any section (**Appearance & Themes**, **Keyboard Height**, **Sound & Haptic Feedback**, **Typing & Correction**, **Personal Dictionary**) — all preferences and your active `T9` / `ABC` / `123` mode persist automatically, and you can toggle **Show app icon in launcher** if you want an icon on your home screen.
